# Precision chart: retired parallel channels

## Findings

- The 418660 daily channel used September reaction lows. Its rails were parallel,
  but the price was already below its lower rail at confirmation on October 2.
  Extending that historical channel to November incorrectly supplied a current
  pullback-support price above the market.
- The same lifecycle defect affected the supplied MSFT weekly channel after its
  upper break on July 31. Six supplied daily/weekly/monthly frames were checked;
  this is not a claim that every watchlist chart was manually reviewed.
- Daily/weekly/monthly trends describe different horizons. A monthly rising
  channel need not imply that the daily trend is rising or that buying is allowed.

## Scope

- `prepare_channel_display` classifies confirmed counter-direction rail breaks
  only for display. Original rail coordinates, pivots, patterns, historical
  events, technical scores, decision gates, allocation and R/R remain unchanged.
- Retired rails end at the first observed confirmed break, are dashed gray, and
  cannot supply current entry/support/resistance guides. Returning inside does
  not revive that old formation. No replacement channel is forced.
- An unconfirmed last bar or quote-only row cannot retire a channel. A pending
  counter-direction break does suspend its use as a current guide.
- Only lower/middle/upper rails remain visible; quarter rails are removed.
- Drawdown explanations no longer deny a separately displayed trend warning.
  Conditional R/R scouting prose explicitly defers to defense/block gates.
- Mixed old/new Cloud chart modules stop chart rendering with a restart notice.

## Checks

- The supplied 418660 daily channel ends October 2; its weekly and monthly
  channels stay active. MSFT daily/monthly stay active; weekly ends July 31.
- Rail slope differences across all six frames are at most 3.7e-12 (roundoff).
- Regression cases cover both directions, immediate/pending breaks, recovery,
  quote-only rows, immutable cached structures, causal prefixes and the supplied
  418660 price sequence (no account data included).
- AST comparison against `3d1934ae` checks that personal-analysis and final-check
  policy expressions are identical except for three explanatory string literals.
- Final full regression run: 5,857 passed, 18 subtests passed, 77 warnings,
  288.77 seconds. The preceding focused chart/provenance run passed 75 tests.
